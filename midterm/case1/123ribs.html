321 RIBS

Fernando Rodriguez

Upgrade your next cookout with our legendary 321 ribs. This simple, foolproof method removes all the guesswork from smoking ribs while delivering incredible, competition-level flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 Rackbaby back pork ribs
  • 1/3 Cupyellow mustard
  • 1/2 Cupapple juice, divided
  • 1 Tablespoon Worcestershire sauce 
  • To Taste Pork & Poultry Rub 
  • 1/2 Cupdark brown sugar
  • * 1/3 Cuphoney , warmed 1 Cup
  • Traeger 'Que BBQ Sauce

Directions

  1. Remove the thin membrane from the back of the ribs by sliding a butter knife underneath it, gripping it tightly with a paper towel, and tearing it off.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the mustard, Worcestershire sauce, and 1/4 cup of apple juice, spread it thinly over both sides of the ribs, and season generously with Traeger Pork & Poultry Rub.
  3. Preheat your Traeger grill to 180°F with the lid closed for 15 minutes, then smoke the ribs meat-side up for 3 hours until the internal temperature hits 160°F.
  4. After 3 hours of smoking, transfer the ribs to a rimmed baking sheet and turn the grill temperature up to 225°F.
  5. Place each rack on heavy-duty foil, top with the brown sugar, honey, and remaining apple juice, then wrap it tightly so no liquid leaks out..
  6. Return the foiled ribs to the grill and cook for an additional 2 hours, or until internal temperature reaches 205℉.
  7. Unwrap the ribs, discard the foil, and brush both sides with Traeger 'Que Sauce before grilling them directly on the grate for 30 to 60 minutes until the sauce tightens.
